Sample corpus (fiction only)

Small set of legal-style notes used as sample data for the Q&A API. All names,
parties, courts, statutes, and facts are invented — nothing here is real legal
material or advice.

Replace these files with your own markdown documents and re-run:

    python ingest.py

Files:
- 01_matter_memo_arvind_v_northfield.md   internal case memo (dates, limitation period)
- 02_employment_agreement_excerpt.md      notice period, non-compete, confidentiality
- 03_hearing_notice_template.md           filing deadlines, sample cause list
- 04_statute_style_excerpt_fictional.md   mediation and interest provisions
- 05_counsel_notes_settlement.md          settlement call notes
- 06_property_lease_clause.md             rent, deposit, subletting
